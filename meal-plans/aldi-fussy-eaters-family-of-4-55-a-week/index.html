Sausage and mash with onion gravy

30 min · Kid-friendly · Air fryer friendly

How to make it

You'll need

  • 6½ sausages
  • 800g potatoes
  • 250g peas
  • 50ml milk
  • 30g spread
  • 1 onion

Plus from the cupboard: cooking oil, garlic (or garlic granules), plain flour, stock cubes, soy sauce, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Boil the peeled potatoes in salted water for 15 to 20 minutes. Drain and mash with the milk, spread and a pinch of salt.
  2. Meanwhile, fry the sausages in 1 tbsp oil over a medium heat for 12 to 15 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through. (Or cook them in the oven at 200°C (180°C fan) for 25 minutes, or the air fryer for 12 to 15.) Keep the sausage fat in the pan.
  3. For the gravy, fry the thinly sliced onion and a crushed garlic clove in the sausage fat for 8 to 10 minutes until soft and golden. Stir in 2 tbsp plain flour and cook for a minute.
  4. Slowly stir in 500ml hot water with a beef stock cube, plus a splash of soy sauce and some pepper. Bubble for 2 to 3 minutes until it coats a spoon. It thickens more as it stands.
  5. Cook the peas and serve everything with lots of gravy.

Fussy eaters: Put the gravy in a jug so everyone adds their own.

Creamy chicken and broccoli pasta bake

40 min · Kid-friendly · Freezes well

How to make it

You'll need

  • 250g chicken breasts
  • 250g pasta
  • 1 head of broccoli
  • 100g mushrooms
  • 200ml milk
  • 20g spread
  • 80g cheese

Plus from the cupboard: plain flour, stock cubes, garlic (or garlic granules), dried mixed herbs, mustard, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Cook the pasta for 3 minutes less than the pack says, adding the broccoli in small florets for the last 3 minutes. Drain.
  2. Meanwhile, cut the chicken into small pieces and fry in the spread for 5 minutes. Add the mushrooms and 2 crushed garlic cloves for 3 minutes.
  3. Stir in 3 tbsp flour for a minute, then slowly stir in 300ml hot stock (1 cube) and the milk. Simmer for 5 minutes until thick. Add 1 tsp herbs, ½ tsp mustard, half the cheese, salt and pepper.
  4. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Mix everything in an ovenproof dish, scatter over the rest of the cheese and bake for 15 minutes until golden and bubbling.

Got leftover roast chicken? Use it instead and skip the frying.

Fussy eaters: Cut the broccoli into tiny 'trees' so it hides in the sauce.

Baked fish in lemon garlic sauce with potatoes

30 min · Kid-friendly

How to make it

You'll need

  • 475g white fish
  • 125ml crème fraîche
  • 25g spread
  • 800g potatoes
  • 250g peas

Plus from the cupboard: garlic (or garlic granules), mustard, lemon juice, dried mixed herbs, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Defrost the fish. Boil the potatoes in chunks for 15 to 20 minutes until soft.
  2. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Put the fish in a baking dish and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Melt the spread with 2 crushed garlic cloves, then mix with the crème fraîche, 1 tbsp mustard and 2 tbsp lemon juice. Pour over the fish and bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the fish flakes easily.
  4. Cook the peas, drain the potatoes and lightly crush them with a fork and a pinch of herbs. Spoon the sauce from the dish over everything.

The sauce makes cheap frozen fish taste like a restaurant dinner.

Fussy eaters: Kids who say they don't like fish often eat it with sauce and mash.

Cheesy sausage pasta bake

45 min · Kid-friendly

Uses the rest of the milk from Tuesday

Uses the rest of the cheese from Tuesday

How to make it

You'll need

  • 325g pasta
  • 20g spread
  • 475ml milk
  • 125g cheese
  • 5 sausages
  • 80g mozzarella
  • 150g peas

Plus from the cupboard: plain flour, mustard, cooking oil, dried mixed herbs, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water for 10 minutes until just tender, then drain.
  2. Melt the spread, stir in 25g plain flour for a minute, then whisk in the milk a little at a time. Simmer for 5 minutes until thick and smooth. Stir in 100g of the cheddar and 1 tsp mustard, and season.
  3. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Mix the pasta into the sauce and tip into a dish.
  4. Fry the sausages, sliced into rounds, in 1 tsp oil for 4 to 5 minutes until browned. Scatter over the pasta with the rest of the cheddar, the torn mozzarella and 1 tsp herbs. Bake for 25 minutes until golden and bubbling. Serve with peas.

Fussy eaters: Pick out a cheesy corner for them. Peas on the side.

Chicken fajitas

30 min · Kid-friendly

Uses the rest of the chicken breasts from Tuesday

Uses the rest of the cheese from Thursday

How to make it

You'll need

  • 400g chicken breasts
  • 2½ peppers
  • 1 onion
  • 6½ wraps
  • 80g cheese
  • 1½ tomatoes
  • 125ml crème fraîche

Plus from the cupboard: cooking oil, paprika, ground cumin, garlic (or garlic granules), lemon juice, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Slice the chicken into strips and mix with 1 tbsp oil, 2 crushed garlic cloves, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tbsp lemon juice, ½ tsp salt and some pepper. Leave for 15 minutes, or up to a day in the fridge.
  2. Get a large frying pan really hot with 1 tbsp oil. Cook the sliced peppers and onion in two batches, leaving them for a minute between stirs so they char at the edges. Season and set aside.
  3. Cook the chicken in the hot pan for 6 to 8 minutes until golden and cooked through. Tip the veg back in and add another squeeze of lemon.
  4. Warm the wraps and fill with the chicken and peppers, grated cheese, chopped tomato and a spoon of crème fraîche.

Fussy eaters: Put everything in separate bowls and let them build their own wrap.

Fish pie

60 min · Kid-friendly · Freezes well

Uses the rest of the milk from Thursday

Uses the rest of the cheese from Friday

How to make it

You'll need

  • 400g white fish
  • 950g potatoes
  • 475ml milk
  • 40g spread
  • 150g peas
  • 40g cheese

Plus from the cupboard: plain flour, mustard, dried mixed herbs, lemon juice, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Defrost the fish. Boil the peeled potatoes for 15 to 20 minutes until soft, then mash with a splash of the milk, a little spread and salt.
  2. Melt the rest of the spread in a pan, stir in 4 tbsp flour for a minute, then whisk in the rest of the milk a little at a time. Simmer for 3 to 4 minutes until thick. Add 1 tsp mustard, 1 tsp herbs, a squeeze of lemon, salt and pepper.
  3. Heat the oven to 190°C (170°C fan). Cut the fish into chunks and put it in an ovenproof dish with the peas. Pour over the sauce and spread the mash on top.
  4. Scatter over the cheese and bake for 25 to 30 minutes until golden and bubbling.

Frozen white fish is a fraction of the price of fresh and works just as well here.

Fussy eaters: Add a tin of sweetcorn to the sauce. Most kids will eat anything under mash and cheese.

Homemade wrap pizzas

20 min · Kid-friendly · Air fryer friendly · Veggie

Uses the rest of the wraps from Friday

Uses the rest of the mozzarella from Thursday

How to make it

You'll need

  • 6½ wraps
  • 1 tin of chopped tomatoes
  • 150g mozzarella
  • 1 pepper
  • 100g mushrooms
  • 1 tin of sweetcorn
  • ½ cucumber

Plus from the cupboard: tomato purée, dried mixed herbs, garlic (or garlic granules), sugar, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). For the sauce, simmer the tomatoes with 2 tbsp purée, a crushed garlic clove, 1 tsp herbs, a pinch of sugar and salt for 5 to 10 minutes until thick.
  2. Spread a thin layer on each wrap and let everyone add their own toppings and cheese.
  3. Bake on trays for 8 to 10 minutes until crisp, or 5 to 6 minutes in the air fryer. Serve with cucumber sticks.

Fussy eaters: Let them choose their own toppings. Kids are more likely to eat what they helped make.

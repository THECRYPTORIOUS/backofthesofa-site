Common questions

Where do the prices come from?

Each supermarket's own website, at normal shelf prices rather than loyalty card or offer prices. We check Aldi every week and the others every month. Prices change, so treat the totals as a good guide.

What does a family of 4 mean here?

Two adults and two children under 11. We count children as smaller portions, about 60% of an adult's dinner.

What isn't included?

Snacks, drinks and household things like loo roll and washing-up liquid. Cupboard basics like oil, stock cubes and herbs are listed but not in the total.

Cheesy sausage pasta bake

45 min · Kid-friendly

How to make it

You'll need

  • 150g pasta
  • 10g spread
  • 250ml milk
  • 60g cheese
  • 2½ sausages
  • 40g mozzarella
  • 80g peas

Plus from the cupboard: plain flour, mustard, cooking oil, dried mixed herbs, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water for 10 minutes until just tender, then drain.
  2. Melt the spread, stir in 25g plain flour for a minute, then whisk in the milk a little at a time. Simmer for 5 minutes until thick and smooth. Stir in 100g of the cheddar and 1 tsp mustard, and season.
  3. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Mix the pasta into the sauce and tip into a dish.
  4. Fry the sausages, sliced into rounds, in 1 tsp oil for 4 to 5 minutes until browned. Scatter over the pasta with the rest of the cheddar, the torn mozzarella and 1 tsp herbs. Bake for 25 minutes until golden and bubbling. Serve with peas.

Fussy eaters: Pick out a cheesy corner for them. Peas on the side.

Cottage pie

60 min · Kid-friendly · Freezes well

Uses the rest of the milk from Monday

How to make it

You'll need

  • 200g mince
  • ½ onion
  • 1 carrot
  • ½ tin of chopped tomatoes
  • 60g peas
  • 400g potatoes
  • 25ml milk
  • 15g spread

Plus from the cupboard: cooking oil, garlic (or garlic granules), tomato purée, stock cubes, dried mixed herbs, worcestershire sauce, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Peel and halve the potatoes and boil in salted water for about 20 minutes until soft. Drain, let them steam for a few minutes, then mash with the milk, spread and a good pinch of salt.
  2. Meanwhile, soften the finely chopped onion and carrot in 1 tbsp oil for 8 minutes. Add a crushed garlic clove, turn the heat up and brown the mince well for 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in 1 tbsp tomato purée, the tomatoes, a crumbled beef stock cube, 1 tsp mixed herbs, a dash of Worcestershire sauce and half a tin of water. Simmer for 15 minutes until thick, then stir in the peas and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Tip into an ovenproof dish, spread the mash on top and rough it up with a fork. Bake at 180°C (160°C fan) for 20 to 25 minutes until golden.

Fussy eaters: Let them fork the mash on top. Kids are more likely to eat what they helped make.

Tomato soup with cheese toasties

30 min · Kid-friendly · Veggie

Uses the rest of the cheese from Monday

How to make it

You'll need

  • 1 tin of chopped tomatoes
  • ½ onion
  • ½ carrot
  • 80g potatoes
  • 40ml crème fraîche
  • ½ loaf
  • 50g cheese
  • 10g spread

Plus from the cupboard: cooking oil, garlic (or garlic granules), tomato purée, stock cubes, sugar, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Fry the chopped onion and carrot in 1 tbsp oil with the lid on for 10 minutes until soft. Add 2 crushed garlic cloves and 2 tbsp tomato purée for a minute.
  2. Add the tomatoes, the potato in small cubes, 700ml hot stock (1 cube) and 1 tsp sugar. Simmer for 15 minutes until the potato is soft.
  3. Blend until smooth, stir in the crème fraîche and season to taste.
  4. For the toasties, fill slices of bread with cheese, spread the outsides thinly and fry in a dry pan for 2 to 3 minutes each side until golden. Cut into soldiers for dunking.

The potato makes it thick and creamy for pennies.

Fussy eaters: Cheese toastie soldiers to dunk win over most kids.

Sausage and mash with onion gravy

30 min · Kid-friendly · Air fryer friendly

Uses the rest of the sausages from Monday

Uses the rest of the milk from Tuesday

How to make it

You'll need

  • 3 sausages
  • 400g potatoes
  • 125g peas
  • 25ml milk
  • 15g spread
  • ½ onion

Plus from the cupboard: cooking oil, garlic (or garlic granules), plain flour, stock cubes, soy sauce, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Boil the peeled potatoes in salted water for 15 to 20 minutes. Drain and mash with the milk, spread and a pinch of salt.
  2. Meanwhile, fry the sausages in 1 tbsp oil over a medium heat for 12 to 15 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through. (Or cook them in the oven at 200°C (180°C fan) for 25 minutes, or the air fryer for 12 to 15.) Keep the sausage fat in the pan.
  3. For the gravy, fry the thinly sliced onion and a crushed garlic clove in the sausage fat for 8 to 10 minutes until soft and golden. Stir in 2 tbsp plain flour and cook for a minute.
  4. Slowly stir in 500ml hot water with a beef stock cube, plus a splash of soy sauce and some pepper. Bubble for 2 to 3 minutes until it coats a spoon. It thickens more as it stands.
  5. Cook the peas and serve everything with lots of gravy.

Fussy eaters: Put the gravy in a jug so everyone adds their own.

Sticky beef mince rice bowls

20 min · Kid-friendly

Uses the rest of the mince from Tuesday

How to make it

You'll need

  • 150g mince
  • ½ onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 125g rice
  • ½ head of broccoli

Plus from the cupboard: cooking oil, garlic (or garlic granules), soy sauce, sugar, honey, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Cook the rice. Steam or boil the broccoli in small florets for the last 4 minutes.
  2. Fry the finely chopped onion in 1 tbsp oil for 3 minutes, add 2 crushed garlic cloves, then turn up the heat and add the mince. Break it up and fry until browned and a bit crispy.
  3. Stir in the grated carrot, then 4 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sugar and 1 tbsp honey. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until sticky.
  4. Serve the beef over the rice with the broccoli.

Grated carrot stretches the mince and soaks up the sauce.

Fussy eaters: Go a bit lighter on the soy for little ones. It's salty.

Mac and cheese with broccoli

30 min · Kid-friendly · Veggie

Uses the rest of the cheese from Wednesday

Uses the rest of the milk from Thursday

How to make it

You'll need

  • 150g pasta
  • 80g cheese
  • 250ml milk
  • 15g spread
  • ½ head of broccoli

Plus from the cupboard: plain flour, mustard, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water, adding the broccoli for the last 4 minutes.
  2. Melt the spread in a pan, stir in 40g plain flour and cook for a minute, then whisk in the milk a bit at a time. Simmer for 5 minutes, stirring, until thick and smooth.
  3. Take it off the heat and stir in most of the cheese, 1 tsp mustard, salt and pepper. Mustard makes it taste cheesier.
  4. Stir in the pasta and broccoli, top with the rest of the cheese and grill until golden. No grill? Put a lid on for a few minutes until it melts.

Fussy eaters: Chop the broccoli tiny, or serve it on the side as little trees.

Roast chicken dinner

110 min · Kid-friendly

How to make it

You'll need

  • ½ whole chicken
  • ½ onion
  • 15g spread
  • 475g potatoes
  • 1 carrot
  • 80g peas
  • 3 Yorkshire puddings

Plus from the cupboard: cooking oil, garlic (or garlic granules), dried mixed herbs, gravy granules, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 minutes before. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Mix the spread with 2 crushed garlic cloves, 1 tsp dried mixed herbs, salt and pepper. Push most of it under the breast skin and rub the rest all over.
  2. Sit the chicken on the halved onion in a roasting tin with a splash of water. Roast for 10 minutes, then turn the oven down to 180°C (160°C fan) for about 1 hour 15 minutes for a 1.5kg bird, until the juices run clear. Spoon the juices over halfway.
  3. Peel and chunk the potatoes, boil for 8 minutes, drain and shake to rough up the edges. Roast in a hot tin with 3 tbsp oil and some salt for 50 to 60 minutes, turning once. Turn the oven up to 200°C (180°C fan) when the chicken comes out to crisp them.
  4. Rest the chicken under foil for 15 minutes. Heat the Yorkshires, boil the carrots and peas, and make gravy with the tin juices, gravy granules and the veg water.

Pick off any leftover chicken for tomorrow's sandwiches.

Fussy eaters: Keep everything separate on the plate and let them pick two veg.

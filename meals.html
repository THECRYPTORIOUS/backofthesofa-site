Common questions

How do you work out the prices?

For Aldi, Tesco, Sainsbury's, Morrisons and Waitrose we use each shop's own website: the cheapest own-brand version of every ingredient, at its normal shelf price (not Clubcard, Nectar or offer prices). We check Aldi every week and the others every month. We price the shop by the pack, the way you pay at the till. If a recipe needs half a bag of spinach, you still buy the whole bag, so we count the whole bag and tell you what's left over. Asda, Lidl and Ocado, and the odd item we couldn't match, are estimated from Which?'s monthly price check of the same basket at each supermarket.

Why is my total less than my usual shop?

The plan covers dinners and, if you choose, breakfast and lunch basics. Snacks, drinks, toiletries and cleaning bits aren't included, so leave some of your budget for those.

Do you have halal meals?

Yes. Tick Halal under "Anything to leave out?" and we'll only pick dinners that can be made halal, with no pork, and price the meat from Tesco's halal range, because Aldi doesn't sell certified halal meat. 65 of our 79 dinners can be made halal. There's also a ready-made £60 halal week for a family of 4.

What if my kids are fussy?

Tick Any fussy eaters? and we'll pick dinners most kids eat and leave out anything spicy. Every recipe also has a fussy-eater tip, like keeping the sauce on the side or letting them build their own.

How big are the portions?

Recipes are written for four adults. We count a child under 11 as about two thirds of an adult portion, and teenagers as adults.

What happens to my answers?

They stay on your phone. There's no account and we never see them. Your plan is still here when you come back.
